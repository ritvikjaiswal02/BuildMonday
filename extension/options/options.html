How to clip

  1. Clip a selection

    Select feedback on any page, including your support inbox, then right-click and choose Clip to BuildMonday. Or press Alt Shift B.

  2. Capture a review page

    On Google Play, the App Store, Reddit, Hacker News, Trustpilot, G2, Product Hunt or X, open the extension and choose Capture. Up to 100 reviews go in at once.

  3. Change the shortcut

    Open chrome://extensions/shortcuts in a new tab.

The extension reads a page only when you clip or capture, and sends only what you clip to your BuildMonday server. Nothing else leaves your browser.
